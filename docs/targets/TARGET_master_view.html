Topic bubbles: three takes

TARGET v1. Approved 2026-10-09: SECTION C (theme tiles) ONLY. A and B are not chosen. Sample themes and titles. Read-only.

A. Soft bubbles, documents as dots

Light tinted circles sized by count, the theme name inside at the top. Each dot is one document. Hover a bubble (shown on "Clients and work") and the dots turn into readable titles. Calmer than v2's solid circles.

B. Bubbles that show their newest three

Solid bubbles like v2, but each shows its three newest titles inside, so you often see the one you want without clicking. Click a bubble for the rest.

C. Theme tiles, sized by count

The same idea with rounded tiles instead of circles: bigger themes get bigger tiles and show more titles. Closest to TileSpace's own tile look, and it uses the screen with no wasted space.
